Even slow breeders outrun their resources

Darwin saw that every species can make more young than its environment can feed. His example, the African elephant, is among the slowest breeders: about six calves in 90 years, half of them female. Each pair leaves three pairs, so numbers triple each generation while the shelf stays the same size.
